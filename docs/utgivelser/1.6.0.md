# Brisk 1.6.0

### New

- **Games** — a new page showing what stands between you and frames per second.
  It reads virtualisation-based security, memory integrity, background recording,
  Game Bar, GPU scheduling and the power plan, and gives each one an estimated
  range: 5–15 % for virtualisation-based security, 1–5 % for background recording,
  −5 to +5 % for GPU scheduling. These are typical measured ranges, not promises —
  what you get depends on the game and on whether the processor or the graphics
  card is the bottleneck.

  Every setting also says what it costs you. Two of them lower your security in a
  real way, and the page says so plainly rather than hiding it behind the word
  "gaming". There is deliberately no button that changes everything at once, and
  no timer resolution, core unparking or SystemResponsiveness — those do nothing,
  and they do not belong in this program.

- **Overview** was rebuilt. Six numbers instead of four, now including drive wear
  and blue screens in the last 30 days, plus a **This machine** panel with your
  Windows build, processor, graphics card, memory, motherboard and uptime.

- **Every list can be sorted** by clicking a column heading; click again to
  reverse it. Sizes sort by what they actually are, so 12 GB comes above 900 MB
  and 1.9 TB above both.

### Changed

- **The cleanup button on Overview now opens Cleanup** instead of deleting
  straight away. It used to run every target marked safe without showing you what
  those were or letting you leave anything out. That is too much to do from a
  summary page.

### Fixed

- **Things no longer fall outside their boxes.** Progress bars ran past the card
  edge, long values overflowed their labels, buttons were drawn on top of text,
  and lists left a dead strip where the columns ran out. All of it had one cause:
  the layout ran before the control knew its real size. Every page scales with the
  window now.
