# Brisk 1.6.4

### Changed

- **Startup separates what is on from what is off.** The list is split into two
  groups with the count in each heading, so what actually starts with Windows is
  one glance instead of a read down the Status column. An entry moves to the
  other group the moment you change it.

- **One button instead of two.** *Turn off selected* and *Turn on selected* have
  become a single button that looks at what you ticked and does the opposite of
  the majority. The label says what will happen and to how many. Entries that
  are already in that state are counted and left alone rather than reported as
  changed.

- **New icons for Cleanup, Maintenance and Tools.** Maintenance and Tools were
  both a wrench, two rows apart in the sidebar, and hard to tell apart. Cleanup
  was three diagonal strokes meant to read as sweeping, which read as a slash.
  They are now a bin, a shield and a toolbox.

- **The sidebar separates updating from repairing.** *Updates* and *Software*
  fetch new versions; *Maintenance* and *Tools* put things right when something
  is wrong. One heading over all four said neither.

### Fixed

- The numbers on the front page sit higher in their fields. The bottom line was
  almost on the edge, which made every field look bottom-heavy.

- Those bottom lines now stand at the same height in all nine fields. Fields
  without a bar had theirs fourteen pixels higher, so reading across the box the
  line wandered up and down.
